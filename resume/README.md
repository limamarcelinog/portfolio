## Summary

I work in Revenue Operations in tech education, connecting marketing, pre-sales, sales and customer success around a single view of the funnel — from first contact to the student's success journey.

My job is to make sure revenue teams have the processes, the metrics and the tools to operate consistently and scale without losing quality. That means funnel modeling, marketing attribution, defining the commercial indicators the company manages by, and building the systems that turn data into decisions.

I do not think the role of RevOps is to report what happened. It is to anticipate the bottleneck, question the premise, and create the conditions for each team to do its best work.

What makes my profile unusual is the range: I model the data in SQL, build the application that consumes it, and have managed the commercial squads that live by the numbers.
